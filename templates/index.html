About NutriCare AI

🤖 Powered by IBM Granite

NutriCare AI leverages IBM Granite — an enterprise-grade large language model from IBM watsonx.ai — to generate personalised, evidence-informed nutrition guidance. Granite is designed for safety, reliability, and responsible AI.

🛡️ AI Ethics & Guardrails

Every request passes through multi-layer guardrails that block harmful content, extreme dietary advice, and medically dangerous recommendations. Sensitive topics trigger enhanced caution warnings. Output is filtered before it reaches you.

💚 HAM Principles

NutriCare AI follows Helpful, Accountable, and Mindful (HAM) communication principles — providing accurate, respectful, and culturally sensitive responses that empower rather than replace professional care.

🌍 Cultural Sensitivity

Nutrition plans are tailored to the user's location, cultural context, and dietary preferences — recommending locally available foods and respecting religious and cultural dietary practices.

🔒 Privacy

Your data is used only within the current session to generate your nutrition plan. No personal information is stored or shared. Sensitive health information is treated with the utmost care.

📋 What NutriCare AI Can Do

  • Generate 7-day personalised meal plans
  • Provide macronutrient & micronutrient guidance
  • Answer general nutrition questions
  • Consider medical conditions in recommendations
  • Suggest locally available, culturally appropriate foods

⚕️ Medical Disclaimer

The information provided by NutriCare AI is for general educational purposes only and does NOT constitute medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. Always consult a qualified healthcare professional or registered dietitian before making significant dietary changes, especially if you have pre-existing medical conditions such as diabetes, hypertension, kidney disease, or cardiovascular conditions.

IBM and the developers of NutriCare AI are not liable for any health outcomes resulting from the use of this information. In case of a medical emergency, contact your local emergency services immediately.
